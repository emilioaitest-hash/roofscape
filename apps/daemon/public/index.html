How work moves through it

  1. You put a goal to it. One sentence, the way you would say it to somebody on their first morning.
  2. The top floor breaks it into tasks — each carrying the terms it will be judged on, agreed before anybody starts.
  3. A floor takes one. Its own worktree, its own budget, its own context. Nobody here holds a conversation; work is a record.
  4. The reviewer reads it against those terms and says whether it holds. It holds nothing that can write, so it cannot quietly fix what it is judging.
  5. Anything leaving the building stops at your desk in the lobby. It begins with you and it ends with you.
