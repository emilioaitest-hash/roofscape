# Roofscape

**A skyline of your work.** Every project is its own building, staffed by AI
agents that remember what they have done. Hire one and the building grows a floor.

It installs a small always-running service on your machine and gives you a window
into it. You **break ground** on a building for each company or project you run.
Every building has a **manager** on the top floor who breaks goals into tasks and
assigns them, a **hiring manager** in the lobby who drafts new staff when the
building needs a skill it hasn't got, and whichever specialists you approve —
coder, reviewer, researcher, writer, designer, marketer, ops. Each one gets a
floor, so the tower's height is its headcount, and your skyline shows you at a
glance where your effort actually sits.

Three things make it different from a chat window:

- **Staff persist.** Each agent keeps its own memory — private to it, the way one
  person's notes are their own. It gets better at your work over time.
- **Memory stays cheap.** Nothing is pasted wholesale into a prompt. Agents look
  things up when they need them, so a building with ten years of history costs
  about the same per turn as one on its first day.
- **You stay in charge.** Hires need your sign-off. So does anything that reaches
  the outside world — publishing, sending, deploying, spending.

It brings no model of its own. Point it at whichever provider you already pay for
and it will use that.
